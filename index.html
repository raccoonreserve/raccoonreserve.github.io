What Is Raccoon Reserve?

Raccoon Reserve ($TRASH) is a fixed-supply ERC-20 community meme token deployed on Base. The project combines raccoon-inspired branding with plans for community engagement and exploration of blockchain recycling concepts.

Proposed future features are ideas under development, not existing services or guaranteed deliverables.

Liquidity and Distribution Disclosure

A Uniswap V3 TRASH/WETH liquidity position exists on Base. The liquidity position is currently creator-controlled and is not locked or burned.

At the initial website preparation stage, approximately 998 million of the one billion TRASH supply remains in the deployer wallet. A broader community distribution has not yet occurred. These figures may change and should be independently checked onchain.

Limited liquidity and concentrated holdings can cause substantial price volatility and trading risk.
